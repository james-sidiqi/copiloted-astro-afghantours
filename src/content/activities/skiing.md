Skiing is a seasonal activity planned from hubs in the locked matrix (Kabul and Bamyan) when snow and access allow.

**Skiing (this activity)** is not the same product as the **Bamyan Skiing Tour**. The tour is a named itinerary with its own page, dates, and quotation path. This activity page covers skiing as planning context you can ask about or weave into a Custom Journey.

We do not invent venues, equipment lists, prices, or schedule guarantees.
