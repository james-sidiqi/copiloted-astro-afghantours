Short walks and day hikes when access and conditions allow — planned from the hub you are based in. Hiking is an activity option, not a packaged mini-tour.

Hub availability is driven by the locked activity×hub matrix. We do not invent venues, prices, or schedule guarantees.

## Planning notes

Activities are inspiration and planning context for Scheduled Tours and Custom Journeys. Specialist logistics and Return Journeys stay on their own routes — they are not reclassified as Activities.
