Trekking here means multi-day mountain travel planned mainly from Faizabad when conditions allow. It is **not** one fixed catalogue trek.

Routes, nights out, and support levels are planned per journey. A named Scheduled Tour that includes trekking (for example a Wakhan corridor itinerary) is a tour product — not a stand-in for this activity page.

Hub availability follows the locked activity×hub matrix. We do not invent venues, prices, or schedule guarantees.

## Planning notes

Activities are inspiration and planning context for Scheduled Tours and Custom Journeys. Specialist logistics and Return Journeys stay on their own routes — they are not reclassified as Activities.
