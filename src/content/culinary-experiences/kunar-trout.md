Kunar's mountain rivers, forested valleys, and roadside cooking traditions make freshly grilled trout one of the province's most distinctive food experiences. Planning sits on the **Jalalabad** hub even when the physical stop is farther up the valley.

## River Valley Food

At riverside restaurants and roadside grills, fresh fish can be prepared simply with salt, spices, heat, and bread. The setting does much of the work: moving water, mountain air, and green valley scenery.

## Exact stop

We do not invent a named restaurant for this page. Exact roadside or riverside venues are confirmed per departure when Kunar access and conditions allow.

## Cultural Pairing: Grilled Trout and Tandoor Naan

Fish from the river, bread from the tandoor, and the landscape around both — one of the most direct food-and-place pairings on eastern routes.
