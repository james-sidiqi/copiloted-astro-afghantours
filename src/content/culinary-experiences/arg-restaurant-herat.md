Arg Restaurant is one of the places guests ask about when Herat is on the route. It sits in the rhythm of the western city — a practical meal stop rather than a staged tourist show.

## Why travelers notice it

Herat’s food culture rewards time spent at local tables. Arg is known among travelers and residents as a reliable place for Afghan dishes after a day of citadel walls, mosque courtyards, and old-city lanes. Menus and specialties change with what the kitchen has; we do not publish fixed prices or guaranteed dishes here.

## How it fits a journey

On Scheduled Tours that include Herat, meal timing follows the day’s access and drive plan. On a Custom Journey, we can build dining around your pace — still without inventing reservations you have not confirmed with us.
